    The building

    Four screens
    From a 60-seat room for small releases to the main hall with a 12-metre screen.
    Sound
    Dolby Atmos in the main hall, so footsteps and thunder come from where they should.
    Seating
    Wide recliners with a drink holder at every seat. Rows are staggered so no one watches the back of a head.
    Access
    Step-free entry, wheelchair spaces in every screen, hearing loops and captioned shows every Saturday.

    Good to know

    When should I arrive?

    Aim for 15 minutes before the start. Trailers run for about 15 minutes, so you won't miss the film if you are a little late.

    Can I cancel or change a booking?

    Yes. Show your reference at the box office at least one hour before the show and we will move or cancel it free of charge.

    What do the ratings mean?

    U is for everyone, PG is fine for children with a parent, 12 and 15 are the minimum ages to watch without an adult. We check ID for 15-rated films.

    Can I bring my own food?

    Please buy snacks at our counter. Water bottles are welcome.
